# Rubric: is this reproduction package ready to post?

## Checks

| Check | Evidence | Pass condition | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| environment-recorded | In eval mode, inspect the repro report's environment/setup record, the issue context, and any relevant Repo facts or repository setup information in the package. In live mode, inspect the student's draft repro comment together with the issue and the repository's README, setup docs, or contribution docs when they name relevant environment requirements. | **Pass** when the repro report identifies the platform/runtime and every version, configuration, branch, commit, dependency, or setup condition that the issue or repository explicitly makes relevant to the reported behavior. If the tested environment differs from the issue's stated environment, the difference is disclosed. **Fail** when an issue-relevant environment condition cannot be determined from the report. | required |
| steps-followable | In eval mode, inspect the repro report's setup and reproduction steps, then compare them with the issue context and any Repo facts or setup instructions in the package. In live mode, inspect the student's draft repro comment together with the repository's documented setup/run instructions and the issue thread. | **Pass** when a stranger can start from the stated setup and perform the documented actions in order without inventing a missing material step. For a successful reproduction, the steps must reach the action or condition that should trigger the reported behavior. For a cannot-reproduce report, the steps must clearly attempt the reported scenario and identify any triggering condition that could not be confirmed or recreated. Commands, inputs, files, routes, settings, or user actions required for the attempted scenario must be stated or unambiguously supplied by the repository's normal documented setup. **Fail** when a material step in the attempted procedure is missing, the steps exercise a different scenario from the issue, or the report claims successful reproduction without actually reaching the required trigger condition. | required |
| behavior-matches | In eval mode, inspect the repro report's artifacts such as terminal output, logs, stack traces, screenshots, test results, or request/response data, and read them against the exact expected and observed behavior in the issue context. In live mode, inspect the evidence included in or linked from the student's draft repro comment and compare it with the live issue. | **Pass** when the artifacts produced by the documented steps show the behavior the issue describes, or support an honest cannot-reproduce result for the same reported scenario. A cannot-reproduce report may pass when the evidence shows a reasonable attempt to exercise the reported behavior, records what actually happened, and clearly identifies any triggering condition that could not be recreated or confirmed. **Fail** when the evidence shows only a different error, adjacent symptom, or unrelated failure, or when the report claims successful reproduction without evidence of the reported behavior. | required |
| outcome-honest | In eval mode, compare the repro report's stated outcome and conclusions with its steps and artifacts, and compare the claim comment with what had been established at claim time. In live mode, compare the student's claim and repro drafts with the evidence they cite. | **Pass** when every factual conclusion is supported by the package's evidence. A report may state reproduced, partially reproduced, or could not reproduce, but the wording must match the observed result. **Fail** if it claims successful reproduction when the evidence shows a different behavior, states an unverified root cause as fact, overstates the scope of testing, or otherwise claims more certainty than the evidence supports. | required |
| repo-conventions | In eval mode, inspect the claim comment and repro report against the package's Repo facts, contribution-policy text, issue/PR template requirements, and any communication rules identified by the package. In live mode, inspect `CONTRIBUTING.md`, linked contributor guidance, dedicated AI-policy files, issue/PR templates, the issue thread, and the student's draft comments. | **Pass** when the comments follow every stated repository convention that applies to issue participation. If the repository requires disclosure of AI assistance for issues or comments, the submitted comment must explicitly include that disclosure and satisfy any required details, such as the tool used and the extent of assistance. **Fail** when the draft violates a stated contribution or communication rule, or when a repository requires an AI-assistance disclosure and the comment omits it, provides only a vague disclosure that does not satisfy the stated requirement, or fails to include required disclosure details. In Path Review live mode, another student's claim does not block posting your own claim, but the student must still provide an independent reproduction rather than piggybacking on another person's result. | required |
| claim-correct | In eval mode, inspect the claim comment against the issue context, the chronology described by the package, and any reproduction evidence included in the package. In live mode, inspect the student's draft claim against the live issue before reproduction work is posted. | **Pass** in eval mode when the claim identifies the specific issue, accurately describes the investigation or reproduction work, and any statement that reproduction has already occurred is supported by evidence available in the package. The claim should also indicate the next investigation step or follow-up when appropriate to its chronology. **Pass** in live mode when the claim identifies the specific issue, states the investigation or reproduction work the student intends to do, and clearly indicates that the student will follow up with the results or findings without asserting an unperformed reproduction. **Fail** when the claim makes an unsupported reproduction claim, promises a fix or completion date, misstates the issue being investigated, or, in live mode, asserts reproduction before that work has been performed. | required |

## Verdict rule

**Accept** a reproduction package if and only if **every required check passes**. **Reject** it if **any required check fails or is unclear**; `unclear` means that the specified evidence is genuinely absent or insufficient after checking the listed sources, not merely that the grader has not looked. The verdict space is binary: `accept` or `reject`. In live mode, apply the Path Review communication rules from `scope.md` in addition to the evidence sources above.
